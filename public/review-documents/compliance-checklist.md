# Compliance Checklist

Owner: Maya Chen
Review due: 2026-08-05
Audience: Compliance Review

## Required Evidence

- Business owner is identified.
- Impacted customer and submission segments are listed.
- Fairness risks are documented.
- Data sensitivity and retention requirements are recorded.
- Monitoring signals and escalation thresholds are defined.

## Review Notes

The reviewer should confirm that the change is limited to triage support, not automated underwriting approval. The reviewer should also verify that missing evidence, high uncertainty, and restricted occupancy classes route to manual review.

## Approval Conditions

- The final workflow must keep approval status visible on each approval node.
- Any rejected item must route to the governance review queue.
- The release owner must provide a rollback plan before production use.
