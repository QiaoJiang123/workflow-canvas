# Legal Terms Review

Owner: Jordan Patel
Review due: 2026-08-07
Audience: Legal Review

## Scope

Legal review covers customer-facing language, vendor obligations, retention commitments, third-party service usage, and audit evidence retention for the underwriting approval chain.

## Questions

- Does the triage workflow change any customer-facing underwriting obligation?
- Are third-party model and OCR providers covered by the current contract language?
- Are extracted document facts retained only for approved business purposes?
- Are rejection and escalation events retained for audit review?

## Draft Finding

The proposed chain is acceptable if the final release notes state that the workflow supports internal triage and does not make binding coverage, pricing, or eligibility decisions.
